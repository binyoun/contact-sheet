Step 1 ▸ Load your film

Artists come from the class list based on Robertson and McDaniel, Themes of Contemporary Art: Visual Art after 1980, from your course materials, and from recent photography prizes and magazines. Artists marked In the library have a book you can borrow at Beanland Library, Saigon South. A match is a starting point, not an answer: look at the real work, read about it, then decide what you can learn from it. Always reference the artists who inspire you.
